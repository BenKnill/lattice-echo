Real case · Vancouver, 1982–83

The index that lost half its value

The Vancouver Stock Exchange started a new index at 1,000 in January 1982 and updated it about 3,000 times a day. Each update truncated it to three decimals, which always rounds down. After 22 months it read 524.811; recomputed properly, it was 1,098.892. Each cycle here is 2.2 months of a miniature market calibrated to those two numbers.

Real case · Dhahran, 25 February 1991

The clock that drifted a third of a second

A Patriot battery counted time in tenths of a second, then multiplied by 1/10 held in a 24-bit register. 1/10 has no exact binary form, so each tick lost 0.000000095 s. After 100 hours of continuous running the tracking gate had moved 687 m. It did not track the incoming Scud, and 28 soldiers were killed. Each cycle here is 10 hours of uptime.
